Research profile

My research centers on lexical semantics within linguistics. Taking an interdisciplinary perspective, I focus on metaphor, nonliteral meaning, and multimodal meaning, combining corpus analysis, computational methods, and large language models to investigate how meaning is represented, constructed, and understood. I specialize in operationalizing linguistic concepts and research questions into clearly defined analytical dimensions and indicators that can be annotated, measured, and computed. Through systematic empirical analysis, I describe and explain linguistic phenomena and test and develop relevant theories.
