Lexical semantics & multimodal meaning

Words, meaning
& perception.

I’m Yufeng Wu, a PhD candidate in the Department of Linguistics and Translation at City University of Hong Kong.

My research centers on lexical semantics, with a focus on metaphor, nonliteral meaning, and multimodal meaning. I combine corpus analysis, computational methods, and large language models to investigate how meaning is represented, constructed, and understood.

Research interests

Lexical meaning

Meaning representation, construction, and understanding, approached through corpus analysis and computational methods.

Metaphor & nonliteral language

Metaphorical meaning in Chinese, metaphor identification, and the use of large language models in linguistic analysis.

Multimodal semantics

Relationships between linguistic meaning and perceptual information, with a particular interest in color and sensory experience.

Across these areas, I operationalize linguistic concepts into clearly defined dimensions that can be annotated, measured, and computed.
